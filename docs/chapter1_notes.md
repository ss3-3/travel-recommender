# Chapter 1 Notes

## Project Theme

Travel Multi-Destination Recommendation System


## Original Problem Idea

Traditional travel recommenders recommend single destinations.


## Final Research Problem

Existing TRS often provide isolated POI recommendations.
Need to investigate whether traditional recommender approaches can support multi-destination travel recommendation.


## Why CBF?

Because destination characteristics are available:
- category
- province
- city
- attraction level


## Why CF?

Because dataset contains:
- tourist_id
- attraction_name
- rating


## Why not route optimization?

Out of project scope.
No latitude/longitude.
Focus is recommendation performance.
